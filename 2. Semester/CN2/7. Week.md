### Availability
**Availability** is the most important thing

MTBF = Mean Time Between Failures = uptime
MTTR = Mean Time To Repair = Downtime

$availability = {MTBF\over MTBF + MTTR}$

#### MTBF
Mean (expected) amount of time between a network becoming operational and the next failure.

#### MTTR
Mean (expected) amount of time it takes to:

**MTTD**: Mean Time To Detect
**MTTI**: Mean Time To Identify
**MTTR**: Mean Time To Repair (Resolve)
**MTRS**: Mean Time To Restore Service
**MTBSI**: Mean Time Between Service
Incidents
**MTBF**: Mean Time Between Failures

### Topology

#### Hierarchical model
##### Core
Backbone of the network
Only on layer 3
##### Distribution
Aggregate data from multiple access switches and connect to the core
Avoid Layer 2 because there is no loop protection and spanning tree blocks ports
##### Collapsed Core
Core and distribution combined
Reduces complexity but also removes scalability options.
##### Access
Connects end users to the network
Layer 2 features here
