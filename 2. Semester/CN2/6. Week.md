## Multicast
One client wants to talk to multiple specific other devices
Routers actively participate in multicast, making copies of packets and forwarding them to the receivers.
Multicast is **UDP** based
#### Multicast source
Multicast sends to one multicast IP address

#### Multicast IP Address Mapping
1110(Multicast prefix, always starts with that) 1111 1(Variable) 1111111 00000000 00000001

Multicast trees are initiated by receivers signaling their request to join a group

#### Source-based tree (S, G)
Notation: (SG1, G), (SG2, G)
	S1 = Source 1
	S2 = Source 2
	G = Group

#### Shared Tree (\*,G)
Notation: (\*,G)
	\* = All Source
	G = Group
