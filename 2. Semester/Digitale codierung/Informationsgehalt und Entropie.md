## Entropie
$H=\displaystyle\sum p(x)I(x)$
- $A=0.5 \cdot 1 = 0.5$
- $B=0.25 \cdot 2 = 0.5$
- $C=0.125 \cdot 3 = 0.375$
- $D=0.125 \cdot 3 = 0.375$
$H=0.5 + 0.5 + 0.375 + 0.375 = 1.75Bit$
