## Identity Management
**Right people** can access the **right resources** for the **right reasons** at the **right time**

### Access Control
##### Physical
guards, fences, cameras
##### Technical or logical control
authentication methods, encryption
##### Administrative control
policies, procedures, background checks

#### Types of security control
Preventive:
	Fences, locks, IPS
Detective:
	Security guards, IDS, CCTV
Corrective:
	Reboot, Antivirus
