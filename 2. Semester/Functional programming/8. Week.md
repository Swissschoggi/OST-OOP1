## Lambda Calculus

$\color{red}M ::= x | \lambda x. M | M M$
$M=\lambda$-term
$x=$ placeholder
$\lambda x. M=\lambda$ abstraction
$MM=$ function application

Abstraction is left associative:
$M_1 M_2 M_3 = (M_1 M_2)M_3$
